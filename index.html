Research

Three questions we are asking

01

Structure of [system]

Single-particle cryo-EM to resolve conformational states of [complex] and map the transitions between them.

02

Mechanism in context

Cryo-electron tomography and biochemical reconstitution to place structures back into their native cellular environment.

03

Prediction & design

Deep-learning structure prediction, MD simulation and protein design to generate hypotheses that experiments can test.

Approach

Experiment and computation, in one loop

Structure prediction is now good enough to propose models faster than we can test them. That makes the experimental side more valuable, not less — the interesting biology sits exactly where the prediction is uncertain: dynamic regions, transient complexes, and states that only exist under load.

Our lab is built to close that loop quickly. We predict, we prepare grids, we solve, and we feed the result back into the next prediction.
