Open positions

Graduate students (M.S. / Ph.D.)

Admission runs through the Department of Life Science. Before applying, email [id]@hanyang.ac.kr with your CV, transcript, and a short note on which of our projects interests you and why.

Undergraduate interns

Hanyang undergraduates can join for a semester or over the summer. No prior research experience is required — curiosity and consistency matter more.

Postdoctoral researchers

We welcome candidates with a background in cryo-EM, protein biochemistry, or computational structural biology. Please include a CV and a one-page research statement.

What to expect

A new lab means unusual freedom and unusual responsibility: you will help set up methods, and your project will be genuinely your own.

Writing to us

A useful email tells us three things: what you have worked on, which project here you want to work on, and when you would be able to start. Attach a CV. We reply to every message.
